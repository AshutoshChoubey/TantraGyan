Deeptadi Avastha – 9 Planetary States Based on Rashi Placement

The nine states are:

1. Deepta (दीप्त): Planet in its exalted sign, delivering maximum positive results.

2. Swastha (स्वस्थ): Planet in its own sign, indicating comfort and strength.

3. Mudit (मुदित): Planet in a great friend’s sign, promoting joy and prosperity.

4. Shanta (शांत): Planet in a friend’s sign, fostering peace and moderate success.

5. Deena (दीन): Planet in a neutral sign, yielding average results with effort.

6. Dukhi (दुःखी): Planet in an enemy’s sign, causing distress and challenges.

7. Vikala (विकल): Planet in a malefic’s sign, leading to obstacles or negative outcomes.

8. Khala (खल): Planet in a great enemy’s sign, resulting in significant difficulties.

9. Kopi (कोपी): Planet combust with the Sun, weakening its influence.

Mooltrikona Signs

The Mooltrikona signs for each planet are:

Sun: Leo (0-20°)

Moon: Taurus (4-20°)

Mars: Aries (0-12°)

Mercury: Virgo (16-20°)

Jupiter: Sagittarius (0-10°)

Venus: Libra (0-15°)

Saturn: Aquarius (0-20°)

Rahu and Ketu: No Mooltrikona signs, as they do not rule zodiac signs traditionally.
