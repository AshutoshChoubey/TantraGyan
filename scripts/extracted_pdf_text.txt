Dietary Restrictions (Sattvic Āhāra)
Based on Ayurvedic and Tantric dietary guidelines:
1.  Prohibited: Non-vegetarian food, alcohol, tobacco, drugs, onion, garlic.
2.  Food Timing: Avoid heavy meals 90–120 minutes before sadhana.
3.  Freshness: Food should not be more than 3 hours old.
4.  Taste: Avoid excessively spicy, sweet, or bitter foods.
5.  Quality: Only pure, homemade sattvic preparations.
Brahmacharya (Complete Celibacy)
During sadhana, maintain complete celibacy:
1.  Physical: No sexual activity.
2.  Mental: Avoid romantic or lustful thoughts.
3.  Visual: Avoid romantic or erotic content.
Communication Discipline (Mauna)
1.  Speech: Only essential, work-related communication.
2.  Phone Usage: Limited to urgent calls and essential work.
3.  Entertainment: No movies, music, social media, or news.
4.  Content: Avoid negative or stimulating material.
Physical Austerities
1.  Ground Sleeping: Mandatory for 41 days.
2.  Same Location: Perform sadhana at a ﬁxed place daily.
3.  Fixed Asana: Use the same mat and posture every night.
4.  Straight Spine: Maintain erect posture.
5.  No Interruptions: Complete silence during practice.
