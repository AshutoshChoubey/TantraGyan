# Design System Baseline — TantraGyan

## 1. Visual Theme & Palette
TantraGyan employs a sacred, scholarly palette evoking ancient parchment, temple stone, sacred saffron, and cosmic night skies:

- **Sacred Gold Accent:** `#d97706` / `#b45309` (Amber / Golden Temple Ochre)
- **Sacred Vermillion:** `#b91c1c` / `#991b1b` (Sindoor / Ritual Crimson)
- **Deep Obsidian Background:** `#090d16` / `#0f172a`
- **Parchment Surface:** `#fef3c7` / `#fffbeb` / `#f8fafc`
- **High-Legibility Foreground:** `#f8fafc` (Dark mode) / `#1e293b` (Light parchment)

## 2. Typography Standard
- **Headings & Sanskrit Titles:** Traditional serif / Devanagari typography (`Rozha One`, `Cinzel`, `Devanagari MT`, `Kohinoor`).
- **Body Text:** High-legibility editorial typefaces (`Merriweather`, `Noto Serif Devanagari`, `system-ui`).
- **Telemetry & Astrological Degrees:** Clean monospace numbers (`SF Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`).

## 3. Structural Rules
- **No Neon AI Halos:** Avoid saturated neon glow box-shadows (`rgba(255, 0, 128, ...)`) that look like generic cyberpunk landing pages.
- **No Clashing Side Tabs on Rounded Boxes:** Use clean 1px structural borders and subtle background tints instead of 4px left-stripes on 12px rounded cards.
- **Crisp Solid Typography:** Headings must be crisp and solid; eliminate gradient text clipping that reduces readability.
