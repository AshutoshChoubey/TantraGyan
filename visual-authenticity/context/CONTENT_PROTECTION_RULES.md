# Content Protection Rules — TantraGyan

## 🚨 MANDATORY INVARIANTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

1. **SACRED SCRIPTURAL & BOOK CONTENT IMMUNITY:**
   - **Zero Alterations to Texts:** All scriptural texts, Sanskrit shlokas, Hindi translations, English commentaries, chapter titles, planetary tables, astrological calculations, and rituals are 100% immune from deletion or modification.
   - **PDF Books Protection:** Never alter, rename, or delete the published PDF editions (`tantra-gyan-vedic-jyotish-mahagranth.pdf`, `bhrigu-nandi-nadi-karma-samyojan.pdf`, `tantra-sadhana-siddhi-protocol.pdf`).
   - If even one sentence or table cell is altered, the spiritual and technical coherence of the book is compromised.

2. **3D BOOK-OPENING ANIMATION IMMUNITY:**
   - The opening book 3D perspective animations (`perspective`, `rotateY`, `transform-style: preserve-3d`) are an essential branded feature of the application.
   - Under NO circumstances should the opening book animation be removed or flattened.

3. **ACOUSTIC AUDIO FEEDBACK IMMUNITY:**
   - The page-turning audio effects and sound synthesis logic in `js/sound.js` and `js/sounds-data.js` must remain completely intact.

4. **KUNDALI CHARTS & SACRED GEOMETRY IMMUNITY:**
   - All North Indian diamond Kundali charts, South Indian charts, Yantras, and celestial glyphs must be preserved without distortion.

5. **ALLOWED REMEDIATIONS:**
   - Removing generic AI "vibe coded" CSS:
     - Gradient text clipping that damages legibility.
     - Asymmetrical left-border side-tabs on rounded containers.
     - Overly saturated neon halo glows.
     - Low-contrast UI control elements (enhancing contrast to meet WCAG AA).
     - Consolidating redundant CSS and removing unused AI boilerplate styles.
