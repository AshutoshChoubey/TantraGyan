## 1. Mathematical Formulas to Demystify
1. **Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (The 5 Great Royal Combos):**
   - Condition: Planet must occupy Kendra (Houses 1, 4, 7, 10) in own sign ($Own$) or exaltation ($Ucha$).
   - Mars $\implies$ Ruchaka Yoga (Courage, land, military leadership)
   - Mercury $\implies$ Bhadra Yoga (Genius mathematics, intellectual mastery)
   - Jupiter $\implies$ Hamsa Yoga (Spiritual wisdom, revered advisor)
   - Venus $\implies$ Malavya Yoga (Art, luxury, unmatched grace)
   - Saturn $\implies$ Sasa Yoga (Mass authority, discipline, industrial empire)
2. **Kendra-Trikona Raja Yogas:**
   - Kendras (1, 4, 7, 10) are Vishnu Sthanas (Action & Protection).
   - Trikonas (1, 5, 9) are Lakshmi Sthanas (Divine Grace & Fortune).
   - Whenever a Kendra lord associates (conjunction, mutual aspect, or parivartana exchange) with a Trikona lord, a supreme Raja Yoga is born!
3. **Dhana Yogas (Wealth Accumulation Engines):**
   - Mutual relationship between 1st (Self), 2nd (Treasury), 5th (Speculative intellect), 9th (Fortune), and 11th (Gains) lords.
4. **Neechabhanga Raja Yoga (Cancellation of Debilitation):**
   4 classical conditions where a fallen planet turns into a king-maker:
   - Dispositor of the fallen planet is in Kendra from Lagna or Moon.
   - Lord of the planet's exaltation sign is in Kendra from Lagna or Moon.
   - The fallen planet is aspected by its sign lord.
   - The planet occupies an exalted Navamsha (D9).
5. **Vipareeta Raja Yogas (Triumph through Adversity):**
   - Harsha (6th lord in 6, 8, 12), Sarala (8th lord in 6, 8, 12), Vimala (12th lord in 6, 8, 12).
