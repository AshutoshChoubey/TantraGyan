## 1. Book Overview & Vision
- **Flagship Identity:** Flagship Vol 4 in Tantra Gyan Sacred Digital Library (`https://t.worldgyan.com`).
- **Core Premise:** The celestial universe does not operate on arbitrary superstition—it is a hyper-precise, deterministic cosmic mathematical engine. This book demystifies every single calculation, formula, algorithm, and combination used in Vedic Astrology (from Swiss Ephemeris Julian Days to 16 Varga charts, 337 Ashtakavarga bindus, 120-year Dasha trees, and 85 marriage rules) into **humanized, layman storytelling with point-to-point clarity**.
- **Target Audience:** Accessible to anyone without mathematical or astronomical background, yet mathematically rigorous enough for senior Vedic astrologers and computational researchers.

## 2. Narrative Voice & Style Guide (Zero-AI Invariant)
1. **The 6-Part Formula Structure:**
   - **Part 1: The Real-Life Story / Parable (रोचक कथा एवं लौकिक दृष्टांत):** A vivid real-world metaphor (e.g. 12 rooms of a royal palace, travelers carrying baggage, clockwork pendulums) establishing the core concept.
   - **Part 2: The Cosmic Rule in Plain Words (सरल शब्दों में ब्रह्मांडीय नियम):** Plain, conversational explanation without jargon.
   - **Part 3: Step-by-Step Simple Math (कदम-दर-कदम सरल अंकगणित):** Elementary school addition, subtraction, division, and modulo operations. No intimidating Greek letters without immediate simple translations.
   - **Part 4: Real Worked Kundali Example (सटीक कुण्डली गणना उदाहरण):** Concrete calculation demonstrated using the benchmark native chart (जातक 'क' / Reference Native Alpha: 28 Oct 1994, 03:45 AM IST, Garhwa Jharkhand).
   - **Part 5: Visual SVG Diagram / Chart (सटीक वैदिक डायग्राम एवं सारणी):** Visual mapping, North Indian diamond chart, or comparison matrix.
   - **Part 6: Life Application & Predictive Takeaway (जीवन में फलित प्रभाव एवं उपयोग):** How this formula directly influences career, wealth, health, marriage, and timing of events.

2. **Tone Invariants:**
   - **Zero Childish Analogies:** No waiter/chef or toy analogies; use noble, traditional, and real-world natural analogies (astronomical clocks, compasses, royal courts, architectural blueprints).
   - **Zero Disclaimers:** Strictly no phrases like "This is just for entertainment", "Astrology is a belief system", or "AI generated summary".
   - **Bilingual Terminology:** Authentic Sanskrit terms with immediate clear English definitions in brackets.
