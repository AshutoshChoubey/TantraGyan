## 1. Mathematical Formulas to Demystify
1. **The 337 Cosmic Points Distribution (Sarvashtakavarga - SAV):**
   - Sun (48 bindus), Moon (49 bindus), Mars (39 bindus), Mercury (54 bindus), Jupiter (56 bindus), Venus (52 bindus), Saturn (39 bindus) = **337 Bindus Total**.
   - Average per house: $337 / 12 = 28.0833 \approx \mathbf{28\text{ Bindus}}$.
   - Auspicious threshold: $\ge 28$ points (supportive, easy gains).
   - Challenging threshold: $< 25$ points (requires immense effort, health caution).
2. **Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV):**
   Each of the 7 planets contributes benefic points (Bindus) to 12 signs from 8 reference points (7 planets + Lagna).
3. **Trikona Shodhana (First Reduction — Triplicity Cleansing):**
   For the 4 elemental triads (Fire: 1-5-9, Earth: 2-6-10, Air: 3-7-11, Water: 4-8-12):
   - Find minimum bindu count in the trine: $M = \min(B_1, B_2, B_3)$.
   - Subtract $M$ from each: $B'_i = B_i - M$.
4. **Ekadhipatya Shodhana (Second Reduction — Dual Lordship Balancing):**
   Balancing signs ruled by the same planet (Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) when signs are unoccupied by planets.
5. **Shodhya Pinda (The Concentrated Karmic Quotient):**
   $$\text{Shodhya Pinda} = \text{Rashi Pinda} + \text{Graha Pinda}$$
   Used to compute the exact year and month of major life milestones, property acquisition, and longevity.
6. **Kakshya System (8 Sub-divisions of a Sign):**
   Each $30^\circ$ sign is divided into 8 Kakshyas of $3^\circ 45'$ each, governed successively by Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, and Lagna. A transiting planet gives fruit only in the Kakshya where it contributed a bindu!
