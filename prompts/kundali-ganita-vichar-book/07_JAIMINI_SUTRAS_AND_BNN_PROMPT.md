## 1. Mathematical Formulas to Demystify
1. **7 Chara Karakas (Movable Role Signifiers):**
   Sort the 7 physical planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) strictly in descending order of degrees traversed in their signs ($d_i = \lambda_i \pmod{30^\circ}$):
   - **Atmakaraka (AK):** Highest degree $\implies$ Soul king, true destiny, spiritual guide.
   - **Amatyakaraka (AmK):** 2nd highest $\implies$ Prime minister, career, profession, status.
   - **Bhratrukaraka (BK):** 3rd highest $\implies$ Siblings, gurus, guides, advisers.
   - **Matrukaraka (MK):** 4th highest $\implies$ Mother, emotional anchors, land, conveyances.
   - **Putrakaraka (PK):** 5th highest $\implies$ Children, creative intellect, disciples.
   - **Gnatikaraka (GK):** 6th highest $\implies$ Obstacles, cousins, rivals, illness, litigation.
   - **Darakaraka (DK):** Lowest degree $\implies$ Spouse, partner, intimate relationships.
2. **12 Arudha Padas Projection Algorithm:**
   For any house $H$ ruled by planet $L$ located $n$ houses away:
   $$\text{Arudha Pada } P = L + n \pmod{12}$$
   - **Crucial Exception Rules:** If the projected Pada falls in the house itself ($n = 0$) or the 7th from it ($n = 6$), advance by 10 houses ($P = (P + 9) \pmod{12} + 1$).
   - **Arudha Lagna (AL):** Reflection of the self in the world (Maya / Public image).
   - **Upapada Lagna (UL):** Arudha of the 12th house (Marriage, spouse's family background, commitment longevity).
   - **Dara Pada (A7):** Business partners and physical romantic attraction.
3. **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) Directional Trines (1-5-9, 2-6-10):**
   - **East (Fire: 1, 5, 9):** Aries, Leo, Sagittarius $\implies$ Dharma Trine.
   - **South (Earth: 2, 6, 10):** Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn $\implies$ Artha Trine.
   - **West (Air: 3, 7, 11):** Gemini, Libra, Aquarius $\implies$ Kama Trine.
   - **North (Water: 4, 8, 12):** Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces $\implies$ Moksha Trine.
   - Planets in the same directional trine act as if conjunct with 100% mutual coordination!
4. **12-Year Jupiter Transit Progression:**
   Jupiter (Jeeva Karaka / Life Principle) traverses one sign every year, completing its 12-year cycle over natal planets to unlock karmic events.
