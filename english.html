Astrologer Ashutosh Kumar Choubey is committed to liberating ancient Vedic knowledge from superstition and fatalistic dogma, presenting it as a rational, verifiable, and deeply empowering science of human consciousness.

Through deep research into authoritative treatises—including Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Saravali (Kalyan Verma), Phaladeepika (Mantreshwara), Jataka Parijata, Nandi Nadi, and Lal Kitab—he formulated reproducible principles applicable to modern charts.

🎯 Predictive Philosophy & Karmic Principles
Core Vision

Ashutosh Ji views the horoscope not as an unalterable fate, but as an intricate cosmic blueprint of accumulated karmas (Sanchita Karma) and ripe destiny (Prarabdha).

By scientifically analyzing planetary dashas, transits, and house significations, an individual can identify inherent potentials, prepare for cycles of challenge, and execute conscious, corrective karma (Kriyamana).

Deeptadi Avastha – 9 Planetary States Based on Rashi Placement

The nine states are:

1. Deepta (दीप्त): Planet in its exalted sign, delivering maximum positive results.

2. Swastha (स्वस्थ): Planet in its own sign, indicating comfort and strength.

3. Mudit (मुदित): Planet in a great friend’s sign, promoting joy and prosperity.

4. Shanta (शांत): Planet in a friend’s sign, fostering peace and moderate success.

5. Deena (दीन): Planet in a neutral sign, yielding average results with effort.

6. Dukhi (दुःखी): Planet in an enemy’s sign, causing distress and challenges.

7. Vikala (विकल): Planet in a malefic’s sign, leading to obstacles or negative outcomes.

8. Khala (खल): Planet in a great enemy’s sign, resulting in significant difficulties.

9. Kopi (कोपी): Planet combust with the Sun, weakening its influence.
