Deeptadi 9 States: Planetary Conditions & Potencies

Deeptadi 9 States (Planetary Conditions & Potencies)
Parashari Sutras

According to Maharishi Parashara, a planet operates through 9 distinct psychological and energetic states based on its zodiacal placement:

  1. Deepta (Radiant / Exalted): Placed in its exaltation sign; delivers maximum auspicious and powerful results.
  2. Swastha (Comfortable / Own Sign): Placed in its own sign; indicates natural strength, stability, and ease.
  3. Mudita (Delighted / Great Friend): Placed in a great friend's sign; bestows happiness, joy, and fortune.
  4. Shanta (Peaceful / Friend Sign): Placed in a friendly sign; promotes harmony, cooperative endeavors, and calm.
  5. Deena (Humbled / Neutral Sign): Placed in a neutral sign; yields moderate, average, and ordinary outcomes.
  6. Dukhi (Distressed / Enemy Sign): Placed in an enemy's sign; brings struggle, psychological friction, and delays.
  7. Vikala (Agitated / Malefic Affliction): Severely afflicted or associated with malefic planets; creates anxiety and restlessness.
  8. Khala (Malicious / Debilitated): Placed in a great enemy sign or debilitation; causes hindrances, setbacks, and adversity.
  9. Kopi (Combust / Enraged): In close combustion with the Sun; significations are scorched and rendered inward.
